## Core Mechanics and Controls: Navigating the River Currents

At the heart of the **otterly lost demo** lies a fluid, momentum-based swimming model. While land movement feels deliberate and slightly waddling—befitting a semi-aquatic mammal—entering the water completely transforms your mobility. Players can glide smoothly on the surface, perform swift underwater dives to bypass surface blockages, and utilize tail kicks for short bursts of directional speed.

Water current plays a dynamic role rather than serving as a passive backdrop. Downstream flows carry your otter effortlessly, reducing stamina consumption, whereas swimming directly against a torrent requires careful pathing through backwater eddies. Mastering the transition between floating on your back to conserve energy and diving to inspect the riverbed is vital for uncovering secret pathways.
