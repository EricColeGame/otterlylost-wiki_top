First, pay attention to the soundscape. Bubbling currents and changing aquatic acoustics often signal submerged caverns or hidden passages tucked behind waterfalls. Diving beneath logs or floating along surface currents can unveil tucked-away coves containing special shiny river pebbles. 

Second, experiment with environmental momentum. Finally, make liberal use of resting areas. Constructing or resting in shoreline dens not only saves your position but often shifts the time of day. Exploring the river during twilight transforms the visual palette completely, bringing glowing fireflies and nocturnal wildlife out into the open.
