## Step-by-Step Walkthrough: The "A Hop Away from Home" Questline

The primary storyline involving the **otterly lost bunny** is the multi-part questline titled *"A Hop Away from Home."* This narrative task tests your puzzle-solving skills, requiring you to bridge waterways that the rabbit cannot cross on its own.

Community reports emphasize taking your time during the bridging sequence, as floating driftwood downstream must be wedged firmly into river bottlenecks before the rabbit will trust the makeshift footbridge.

### Stage 1: Establishing Trust
When you first approach the stranded bunny on the Willow Riverbank, it will recoil behind a patch of stinging nettles. Do not attempt to grab or nudge the animal. Instead, open your satchel and place an offering of fresh greens on a flat boulder nearby, then take three steps back into the shallow water.

### Stage 2: Constructing the Driftwood Crossing
The rabbit needs to reach the opposite bank where the meadows stretch toward its warren. As an agile otter, your job is to gather three buoyant river logs caught in nearby eddies:
1. Swim to the upstream whirlpool and dislodge the birch log.
2. Push the log downriver until it lodges against the center boulder.
3. Repeat the process with two smaller cedar branches found near the beaver lodge.
4. Align the timbers to form a stable floating pathway.

### Stage 3: Guiding the Path
Once the bridge is secure, you must guide the bunny across without causing panic. Walking ahead of the bunny and tossing crunchy seeds onto each log step encourages it to advance safely.

Upon guiding the bunny back to the entrance of the Great Oak Warren, a heartwarming cutscene plays where the bunny reunites with its warren mates. Completing this sequence permanently cements your friendship and unlocks the bunny as a visiting camp NPC.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can the otterly lost bunny swim across rivers?
No, the **otterly lost bunny** is completely unable to swim in deep or swift water. If the rabbit touches deep current, it will immediately scurry back to the nearest dry bank. You must build makeshift log bridges or clear shallow stepping-stone pathways to help it cross streams.

### What happens if I accidentally scare the bunny away?
If you sprint too fast or splash water near the bunny, it will temporarily retreat inside the nearest hollow log or bramble patch. Simply walk a short distance away, wait approximately 30 in-game seconds, and approach again in a gentle crawl with a food treat ready in your paws.

### Where can I find Golden Meadow Carrots for the fastest friendship gain?
Golden Meadow Carrots grow exclusively in the soft loamy soil patches located just east of the Old Mill. Look for green leafy sprouts emerging from small dirt mounds. Harvesting them during a morning dew cycle yields the highest quality carrots.

### Does befriending the otterly lost bunny unlock any Steam achievements?
Yes, assisting the bunny and escorting it back to its warren unlocks the hidden achievement *"Safe on Dry Land."* Reaching maximum friendship level with the rabbit also contributes directly toward the *"Woodland Diplomat"* master achievement.
