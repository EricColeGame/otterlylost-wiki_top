### Swimmers and Current Breakers
Creatures like Shelly and Barnaby handle physical obstacles that would otherwise sweep a small otter downstream. When swimming alongside a Current Breaker, your swimmer gains an aerodynamic drafting effect. This slipstream mechanic cuts down stamina drain during prolonged upstream paddles, letting you push through heavy white-water currents without tumbling backward.

### Scouts and Pathfinders
Avian and small rodent companions excel at navigation. Pip perches on your shoulder or circles overhead, signaling submerged rocks, shallow sandbars, and whirlpools long before they appear on your immediate radar. If you are hunting for hidden collectible shells or submerged relics, traveling with a Pathfinder is practically mandatory.

### Builders and Resource Gatherers
Resource Gatherers like Clover and Milo keep your home den thriving. By assigning these companions to forage near your campsite while you embark on deep-river expeditions, you generate a steady passive income of crafting reeds, smooth river pebbles, and seasonal berries.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Otterly Lost Friends

### Can I miss any of the otterly lost friends during a single playthrough?
No companion is permanently missable. If you progress past a major story milestone without rescuing a friend, their distress call remains active in that specific biome. You can swim back upstream or use fast-travel current channels at any stage of the game to locate and rescue them.

### How many companions can follow me at one time?
Under standard exploration rules, you can have one primary companion swimming alongside you. However, unlocking the "Family Raft" den upgrade later in the game permits a secondary small scout (such as Pip or Clover) to ride on your back while a larger swimmer joins you in the water.

### What happens if a companion runs out of stamina during a swift current run?
Companions do not faint or take permanent injury. If a friend runs out of stamina while navigating violent waters, they will retreat to the nearest calm riverbank or resting boulder. You can simply swim over to them to help them catch their breath, or continue on your journey while they safely return to your river den.

### Which companion should I prioritize unlocking first?
Most players benefit most from unlocking Barnaby early in the Timber Shallows. His ability to clear jammed log flumes unlocks vital shortcuts between the upper streams and lower lakes, dramatically cutting down the travel time needed to complete early-game rescue quests.
