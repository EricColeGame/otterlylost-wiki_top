Picking up a stone immediately changes your weight class. The game features an active physics engine where carrying weight reduces swimming speed, increases stamina consumption when swimming upstream, but dramatically speeds up your dive descent.

### Sunken Pressure Switches and Floor Seals

The most frequent puzzle you will encounter involves submerged stone tiles carved with ancient river motifs. When unweighted, these tiles float flush with the floor, keeping water gates closed. 

To activate them:
1. Identify the switch depth and estimate the water current velocity.
2. Locate a medium or heavy stone within the immediate pool.
3. Dive directly above the tile, using the stone’s ballast to counteract the upward push of the water.
4. Drop the stone precisely onto the plate until a mechanical click confirms the latch.

If the current is too fierce, dropping a stone from above will result in the current sweeping it downstream before it hits the plate. In these instances, you must carry the stone while walking along the riverbed floor.

Smart resource management separates casual swimmers from master navigators. Rather than moving stones one at a time over immense stretches of river, use quiet sandbars as waystations.

Follow this systematic setup:
- **Scout the Rapids First:** Swim through the area unencumbered to identify where gates, switches, and obstacles reside.
- **Identify Calm Water Eddies:** Locate circular water currents behind fallen timber or large boulders where items will not float away.
- **Ferry Medium Stones Downstream:** Take advantage of river current speed to drift stones downstream into your designated staging eddy.
- **Anchor Your Inventory:** Place one heavy Basalt Slab atop smaller pebbles in your cache to prevent high-water surges from scattering your stockpile.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Otterly Lost Rock Mechanics

### Why is it necessary in Otterly Lost collect rocks to solve riverbed puzzles?
In Otterly Lost, collect rocks allows you to counteract your natural otter buoyancy. Without holding or carrying weighted stones, your otter will constantly float back to the surface, making it impossible to remain grounded on submerged pressure plates or navigate through high-velocity underwater tunnels.

### Can rocks despawn if they are swept downstream by the current?
No, unique puzzle rocks do not despawn if they are caught in a river current. Community reports confirm that if a key puzzle stone travels down a waterfall or enters an unrecoverable zone, it will gently wash ashore on the nearest sandbar or reset to its original spawning location after a brief delay.

### How do I store my favorite rock permanently?
As you progress through the narrative, you unlock an expanded pouch mechanic that mimics natural otter anatomy. By pressing the inventory interaction key while holding a light River Pebble or keepsake stone, you can stow it safely inside your armpit fur fold, ensuring it stays with you across biome transitions without weighing you down.
