## Community-Sourced Troubleshooting and Optimization

Even well-crafted indie titles encounter small technical hiccups across varied hardware environments. Community reports and player experience shared across gaming forums reveal a few straightforward fixes for the most common launch and graphical glitches:

### 1. Resolving Black Screens on Launch
If the game boots to audio but displays a black screen, the issue typically stems from a missing media feature pack or an unresolved display refresh rate conflict.
* **Force Windowed Mode:** Add `-windowed` to your launcher executable arguments.
* **Update Visual C++ Redistributables:** Ensure both the x86 and x64 versions of the Microsoft Visual C++ runtime are up to date.
* **Disable Third-Party Overlays:** Turn off conflicting recording overlays from Discord, GeForce Experience, or RivaTuner Statistics Server.

### 2. Eliminating Micro-Stutter During Rapids
Rapid streaming of new river sections can occasionally cause frame timing spikes on mechanical hard drives.
* **Move Game to an SSD:** Solid-state storage virtually eliminates asset-loading hitches when transiting across biome boundaries.
* **Cap Frame Rates in Driver Software:** Use NVIDIA Control Panel or AMD Software to set a maximum frame rate 3 FPS below your monitor's native refresh rate (e.g., 57 FPS on a 60Hz monitor or 141 FPS on a 144Hz monitor) alongside V-Sync to prevent frame pacing stutter.

### 3. Controller Not Recognized
If your gamepad does not respond upon launch:
* Disconnect secondary flight sticks, racing wheels, or virtual emulation adapters.
* In your launcher settings, verify that your specific controller layout is set to "Default Generic Gamepad" or "Xbox Configuration Support."

Keeping these community-tested solutions handy ensures your gaming time stays focused on guiding your whiskered companion rather than troubleshooting technical hurdles.
