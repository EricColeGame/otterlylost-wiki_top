## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I recover lost stamina quickly when trapped in midstream?
If you are caught far from shore with a draining stamina meter, float onto your back and stop actively paddling. Treading water gently on the surface allows your energy bar to recover, albeit more slowly than resting on dry land or inside a calm eddy. Avoid diving until your energy climbs above 40 percent.

### What is the most reliable way to solve underwater maze caves?
Underwater caves feature glowing algae patches along tunnel ceilings that mark the shortest path toward breathable air pockets. Keep your otter aligned with these bioluminescent markers, and utilize whisker navigation to detect outbound water currents indicating cave exits.

### How does this otterly lost guide recommend handling aggressive water currents?
Never attempt to swim directly against high-velocity white water or narrowing flumes. Instead, dive toward the riverbed where friction reduces current velocity, or ride the current outward toward peripheral eddies behind boulders, using diagonal strokes to cut across the flow.

### Can puzzle items float away permanently if dropped in rapids?
Interactive puzzle objects such as buoyant logs, counterweight stones, and keys will not disappear forever if swept away by water flow. The river mechanics naturally deposit displaced quest items onto the nearest downstream sandbar or eddy basin, allowing you to retrieve them easily without restarting the chapter.
