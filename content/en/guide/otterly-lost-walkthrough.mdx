### Chapter 1: Whispering Shallows

The opening chapter acts as an extended tutorial, teaching you how to dive, handle pebbles, and navigate shallow gravel beds. Begin by swimming downstream through the sunlit shallows until you encounter a fallen birch trunk blocking your way. 

To clear this obstacle, dive beneath the submerged root ball on the left side of the creek bed. Pick up a smooth river stone from the silt using your interact key, surface on the downstream side, and drop the stone onto the hollow reed switch to open the underwater gate. Follow the gentle current southeast into the reed maze, keeping close to the southern bank to collect your first Golden Shell.

### Chapter 2: The Timber Dam and Wetland Maze

Upon entering the wetlands, the water slows to a murky crawl, introducing floating log barriers and complex drainage routes. This stage requires you to manipulate water gates built by industrious beavers.

1. Swim to the central dam structure and inspect the jammed sluice gate.
2. Head north into the flooded willow grove to locate three buoyant wooden wedges.
3. Push each wedge into the water elevator channel located along the western revetment.
4. Dive deep to trigger the release counterweight, which raises the river level by two meters and frees the log jam.

Once the timber clears, watch for swift eddies near the spillway. Riding the main crest allows you to vault directly into the canyon flume, skipping several thorny bramble patches along the shoreline.

### Chapter 3: Roaring Rapids and Canyon Flumes

The third biome tests your reflexes and timing. In this section of our **otterly lost walkthrough**, survival depends on spotting resting zones known as eddies. When navigating white-water flumes, avoid jagged midstream rocks by reading the river tongue—the smooth, V-shaped water pointing downstream between two boulders.

If a rapid throws you toward a foaming hydraulic hole, dive immediately. The bottom current moves slower and allows you to bypass the surface vortex safely. Toward the end of the canyon, you must leap over a natural stone weir by combining a rapid surface dash with an upward breach.

### Chapter 4: Sunken Estuary and the Raft Reunion

The final chapter features massive tidal gates, brackish waters, and shifting currents influenced by the ocean tide. When low tide occurs, previously submerged caves become accessible, revealing pathways beneath ancient stone ruins.

To complete the final gate puzzle, collect two basalt weights from the ocean floor. Place one on the left hydraulic pillar and one on the right counter-lever within 30 seconds of each other. Once both pillars align, the estuary sea doors slide open, allowing you to swim out into the open bay and reunite with your family.

## Pro Tips for Route Planning and Speedrunning

Mastering the movement mechanics allows you to complete levels in a fraction of the standard playtime. Incorporating advanced techniques transforms challenging waterways into effortless speedways.

*   **Breach Surfing:** Diving deep and pointing your snout directly toward the surface at full speed propels your otter high into the air. Use this height to clear floating log booms without solving their associated switch puzzles.
*   **Mud Bank Drifting:** When transiting over land, hold the dive key to initiate a mud belly slide. Sliding down muddy inclines preserves full swimming momentum and allows you to transition smoothly back into the water.
*   **Current Snapping:** Hugging the inside line of river curves minimizes travel distance and keeps you clear of external whirlpools that form along outer bends.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How long does a full playthrough take using an otterly lost walkthrough?

### Can you backtrack if you miss a Golden Shell in an earlier river level?
Yes. After reaching the estuary at the end of the campaign, you unlock the River Map at your home holt. This feature allows you to travel back to any previously visited zone and retrieve any missed Golden Shells, curios, or den decorations without resetting your story progress.

### What should I do if my otter gets stuck behind a closed water gate?
If an underwater gate shuts and traps you without a stone nearby, look for a small silt tunnel near the riverbed. The level geometry always includes emergency return flumes or bypass crevices so players never find themselves permanently soft-locked behind puzzle gates.

### Does swimming against strong currents drain your oxygen meter faster?
While swimming against currents does not directly deplete oxygen, it drains your stamina gauge quickly. Once your stamina empties while submerged, your otter automatically slows down, indirectly reducing the distance you can cover before needing to surface for fresh air.
