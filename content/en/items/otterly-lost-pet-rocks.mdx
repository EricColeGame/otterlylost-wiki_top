## Frequently Asked Questions About Otterly Lost Pet Rocks

### Can I lose my pet rocks if I drop them in fast-moving rapids?
No, the game includes a forgiving recovery system. If you drop any of your **otterly lost pet rocks** in heavy white-water currents, they will gradually wash downstream into the nearest calm pool or eddy, where they will rest safely until you paddle over to retrieve them.

### How many pet rocks can my otter carry at one time?
At the start of your journey, your underarm skin pouch holds up to two small stones. As you explore, forage sturdy reeds, and trade with regional wildlife, you can upgrade your pouch capacity to carry up to five different stones simultaneously.

### Do pet rocks degrade or break when cracking open clam shells?
Standard stones do not break under normal usage. However, delicate rocks like layered shale can wear down if used continuously against heavily armored crustaceans. To preserve your favorite decorative stones, keep a rugged piece of basalt or granite on hand specifically reserved for tool work.

### Are all otterly lost pet rocks required to complete the main storyline?
No, finding every single stone is entirely optional and primarily intended for completionists and collectors. While you will need a few foundational stone types to solve environmental locks and open dams during main story quests, most hidden rocks exist to reward curious swimmers with unique den decorations and fun trick animations.
