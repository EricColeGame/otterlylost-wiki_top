## Frequently Asked Questions About Otterly Lost Shells

### Do otterly lost shells respawn after you harvest them?
Yes, **otterly lost shells** respawn consistently based on their individual rarity tiers. Common varieties like Creek Clams reappear every in-game morning, whereas mid-tier Whirlpool Conchs take roughly three in-game days to regenerate. Rare coastal shells, such as the Luminescent Abalone, operate on a longer multi-day cycle tied to moon phases and tidal shifts.

### What is the rarest shell in the game?
The Luminescent Abalone is widely considered the rarest renewable shell. Found strictly in deep surge channels along the rocky coastline, it only emits its telltale green glow during nighttime low tides. In addition, players can uncover static, one-of-a-kind Sunken Fossils hidden within secret underwater grottos that do not respawn once collected.

### Can you store extra shells when your inventory is full?
Yes. You can craft woven reed storage baskets inside your home den to stockpile your spare shells. Early in the game, your personal pouch space is limited, so depositing valuable shells in your home den prevents overencumbrance while swimming and protects your stash from being lost in turbulent whitewater.

### Why do some river shells refuse to open or dislodge?
Certain shells, particularly ribbed mussels and ancient conchs, adhere tightly to stone surfaces or have impenetrable protective seals. You need to equip a flat river pebble in your active tool slot to pry them loose from rocks or crack them open on the riverbank without depleting your breath meter.
