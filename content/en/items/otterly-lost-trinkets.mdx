## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if I drop an item in fast-moving rapids?
If you drop an item in fast rapids, it will tumble downstream along the riverbed until it hits a natural barrier, such as a beaver dam, boulder eddy, or sandbar. Follow the current downstream to locate and recover it. Items do not despawn if lost in transit.

### Can all otterly lost trinkets be traded away safely?
Most curios can be traded or gifted to river animals, but unique narrative artifacts—marked by a distinct golden glow in your inventory—are permanently protected. Even if an animal requests an essential quest item, trading it will advance your story rather than locking you out of den upgrades.

### How do I reach trinkets stuck beneath heavy submerged logs?
To dislodge items trapped under timber, you can either enlist the help of friendly beavers by trading with them or use river stones as wedges. Alternatively, improving your dive roll maneuver allows you to bump into specific loose logs, shifting them just enough to free the trapped treasure.

### Do buried trinkets respawn after you dig them up?
Natural items like polished river pebbles, colorful river agates, and snail shells slowly respawn after seasonal rains replenish the riverbanks. Unique human relics, antique coins, and handcrafted jewelry are limited collectibles that remain in your den or trade network once discovered.
