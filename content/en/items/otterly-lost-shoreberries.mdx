## Frequently Asked Questions About Otterly Lost Shoreberries

### Do otterly lost shoreberries respawn after being picked?
Yes, shoreberry bushes regenerate their fruit regularly. Most standard wild bushes respawn within one to two in-game days. However, specialized plants—such as Golden Shoreberries or shaded mangrove variants—may take up to three full daily cycles to blossom again.

### Can you plant or cultivate shoreberry bushes near your den?
While you cannot transplant mature shrubs directly, player experience indicates that finding wild seeds inside ripe berry clusters allows you to plant garden plots near your den. Cultivating home plots ensures a steady supply of fresh fruit without needing to travel downriver every morning.

### What is the best recipe to make using otterly lost shoreberries?
For general exploration, the **Glazed River Tart** is widely considered the best balanced dish. It provides a generous instant stamina boost alongside a sustained diving speed enhancement, making it invaluable for navigating rapid currents and deep ocean ravines.

### How do I harvest bushes that are underwater during high tide?
Submerged bushes cannot be plucked using the standard terrestrial foraging action. You must either wait for low tide when the sandbars re-emerge or dive directly above the bush and use the underwater paw-sweep technique to loosen the berries from their stems.
